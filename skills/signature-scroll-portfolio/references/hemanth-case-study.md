# Case study: Inside Hemanth — Think → Build

Use this reference to understand the decisions and reversals behind one cinematic portfolio, not to copy its identity. The reusable method is **personal evidence → visual motif → controlled transitions → actual work → useful website**. An eye, blue light, processor and H² mark are this project's choices, not skill requirements.

Jump to [identity](#2-approve-a-reusable-identity-source-then-fix-the-right-mismatch), [actual Kaggle work](#6-cleanup-and-kaggle-what-actually-ran), [v2 continuity](#7-a-completed-v1-film-still-needed-a-different-continuity-solution), [web delivery](#10-v5v6-traded-all-i-weight-for-measured-adaptive-delivery) or [the choice of silence](#11-a-local-sound-experiment-then-the-choice-of-silence).

## 1. Discover a motif that explains the person

**2 October — approved concept.** The recorded brief connected curiosity, learning, experimentation and useful software through a scale reversal: enter the creator's eye, travel through thought becoming circuitry, reveal work, then return to the same person. “Think → Build” supplied a narrative reason for the transitions. The concept revision replaced an earlier quieter 18-second proposal with a six-beat, 20-second story:

| Original storyboard interval | Intended beat |
| --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Partial-face lighting reveal and approach |
| 3–6 s | Natural iris becomes abstract threads; pupil entry |
| 6–10 s | Neural forms assemble around the camera |
| 10–14 s | Organic paths resolve into circuitry and a processor |
| 14–17 s | Controlled release resolves into project surfaces |
| 17–20 s | Pullback through the world and eye; portrait handoff |

These are **original edit intentions**, not the present scroll-distance allocation. The eye/brain imagery was explicitly a metaphor, not anatomical visualization. One travelling light tied the worlds together. Black and electric azure supported that light; the face and recognizable iris were to remain naturally coloured.

The initial interaction proposal was a one-time, skippable intro followed by ordinary content. Later work changed it to a native-scroll story. Treating the original intro plan as the shipped interaction would misstate the result.

**Lesson:** discover a meaningful transformation and return before choosing effects. Someone else's practice might suggest a sketch becoming a building, a sound becoming a waveform, or a material becoming a product. Private background can inform this choice without becoming a public asset or an image-model attachment. [Concept evidence][concept]

## 2. Approve a reusable identity source, then fix the right mismatch

**2 October — portrait and natural-eye selection.** An externally generated **1122×1402 portrait** became the selected visual source. Its image model was not recorded. It was not pixel-identical to the supplied photograph. Local wide, balanced and tall compositions reused it with uniform scale, placement and edge blending rather than independently regenerating a face for each device.

Four supplied eye photographs were reviewed in the historical work. A more frontal view was initially preferred over a sharper oblique view because continuity mattered more than nominal resolution. The first generated eye still looked sideways. Stage 05 correctly distinguished **gaze relative to lids/corners** from **pupil position on the canvas**: centring a crop cannot redirect gaze.

The revised reference roles made the portrait authoritative for target gaze, perspective and local lighting, with the real macro as detail context. The second corrected candidate became EYE-01; regeneration stopped. Its selection was an aesthetic decision, not biometric verification or proof of authentic reconstructed iris detail.

**Lesson:** record which source controls identity, viewpoint, texture and lighting. Match the local feature, not a distant blue rim elsewhere in the portrait. Preserve originals and reject “sharper” detail that changes the person. [Portrait/framing][portrait] · [gaze correction][gaze] · [EYE-01 selection][eye]

## 3. Build a chain of boundaries, not unrelated attractive images

**2 October — stages 07–14.** Selected stills progressed from iris threads to pupil threshold, neural formation, neural interior, hybrid circuitry, processor, release and blank planes. Adjacent stages reused exact files:

- EYE-03 = MIND-01.
- MIND-03 = TRANSFORM-01.
- TRANSFORM-03 = BUILD-01.
- BUILD-03 = RETURN-01.

Selection notes also preserved deviations: excess gold at the pupil threshold, competing neural highlights, over-bright chip flares and overlapping panel corners. The response was usually a bounded correction in the next stage or deterministic compositing, not restarting every approved asset.

The later **16-panel storyboard** communicated the sequence but was only **1586×992 for the whole sheet**. It was neither a high-resolution frame source nor a temporal constraint for video generation. Its stylized return eye did not replace the approved natural-eye/portrait references. First/last video inputs remained separate, full-resolution, unlabelled assets.

**Lesson:** reuse boundaries by file identity, but inspect the resulting clip ends: matching input references do not guarantee matching generated output frames. A storyboard explains the story; it does not prove the animation exists. [Boundary progression][boundaries] · [return staging][return-plan] · [storyboard review][opening]

## 4. Compare one hard shot, then respect the actual model decision

**2 October — S05 comparison, then Omni-only direction.** Two supplied neural-to-circuit clips were attributed by Hemanth to Seedance and Omni. The editor preferred Seedance for this particular transformation's dimensional progression; Omni had cleaner fine linework but competing light sweeps and a late arrangement change. Inputs, hosts, codecs, resolutions and editorial trims were not controlled, so this was **not a model benchmark**.

Hemanth subsequently chose **Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash** and requested the storyboard and opening first. That superseded the Seedance recommendation. Of two opening candidates, the softer 720p take was selected over a clearer 1080p take because the latter contained a prolonged blink. The selected opening was a working motion reference, not evidence of exact face preservation or necessarily the final portrait treatment.

**Lesson:** motion, identity and endpoint behaviour can outweigh export resolution. Attribute model identity honestly: the early clips relied on user attribution; branding on a returned file did not independently establish the served model. Later production notes recorded the connected Flow UI's Omni settings. [A/B review][comparison] · [Omni decision][omni] · [opening selection][opening]

## 5. Replace test mattes with authored full-frame references

**2–3 October — full-frame production.** Early square-on-black landscape tests deliberately retained the full square reference. They were not accepted as final full-bleed art direction. Stage 19 replaced S01's padded inputs with a deliberate eye-centred 16:9 crop and uniform resize, without stretching or synthesizing new anatomy.

The remaining nine references included a corrected final project arrangement: three separate blank planes above, rather than obscuring, the mounted processor. Flow upload was initially blocked by the browser tool's workspace restriction. The legitimate user upload resolved it; changing paths, injecting bytes or invoking page internals was explicitly excluded.

Later receipts record nine remaining first-pass shots plus three targeted retries: S05 lighting and two S08 reveal repairs. S08's early flare and doubled hardware were rejected; take03 was retained. All ten selected scene candidates were preserved as **1920×1080, 144-frame, 24-fps, six-second** files. S01 was supplied at that raster; the other nine used Flow's **1080p Upscaled** export. Those dimensions do not prove native generated detail.

**Lesson:** a test accommodation must not silently become the final composition. Fix one diagnosed defect per retry and verify the actual download against the selected result. Submission counts are not independently audited billing. [Full-frame correction][fullframe] · [production record][production]

## 6. Cleanup and Kaggle: what actually ran

**3 October — completed processing, not just a GPU proposal.** Stage 21's website-only cleanup handoff was superseded by explicit authorization for local cleanup and Kaggle upscaling. The website picker restriction was not bypassed. Older status paragraphs still say “running” or “not uploaded”; later output receipts and the final technical summary are stronger evidence.

| Method or stage | Evidence-backed outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Private GPU preflight | Completed synthetic GPU inference; no personal media needed for this check. Allocation reported two T4s, not proof of two-GPU scaling. |
| Reverse-alpha / CPU temporal cleanup pilots | Ran and were rejected for visible outlines or damaged detail. Not final inputs. |
| Twelve-frame upscale comparison | Ran on six eye and six circuit frames. These were **uncleaned diagnostic samples**, explicitly prohibited as final media. |
| Upscale choice | Lanczos4 for eye-containing S01/S02/R02. For neural/circuit shots: general-x4v3 at DNI 0.2, then a **50% whole-image blend with Lanczos**. The blend is not DNI 0.5. x2plus was tested but not selected. |
| LaMa corner pilot | Ran on twelve actual before/after samples; conditional approval for full processing, not whole-film approval. |
| Full batch | Completed **ten source clips**, producing **twenty FFV1 intermediates**: cleaned1080 and upscaled3840×2160 per clip. The earlier suggestion to process only the edited 480 frames was not what this batch did. |
| Local verification | Recorded decoding of **1,440 frames at each resolution**, matching processed-RGB hashes and unchanged original hashes. Cleaned1080 had zero changes outside the specified mask. |
| S03 repair | A cleanup-induced cyan spike at source frame 074 received a narrowly masked adjacent-frame estimate and was integrated after review. |
| Other research | BasicVSR++, RealBasicVSR, Video2X and optional RealESRNet were discussed as alternatives, not established full-run methods in this evidence. |

The approved cleanup mask covered **3,399 pixels**, about **0.164%** of a 1080p frame, using a 512-pixel context and inside-only feathering. Unchanged pixels outside that mask do **not** prove the occluded content was recovered. The repair estimates hidden pixels; neural enlargement can infer texture.

FFV1 preserved the **processed RGB samples** losslessly. It did not reverse the provider's lossy encoding, restore hidden pixels exactly or create native 4K photography. No face enhancement or generated intermediate frames were part of the selected processing chain. Runtime extrapolations in pilot reports were not full-batch timing measurements.

Private notebooks/datasets and internet-disabled execution were recorded. Their account identifiers and private links are deliberately omitted here. Historical quota observations are not a reusable allocation promise. [Processing notes][finishing] · [pilot selection][pilot] · [batch report][batch] · [independent technical summary][technical]

## 7. A completed v1 film still needed a different continuity solution

**3 October — v1 to v2.** The v1 480-frame film was assembled with an intentional registered portrait/macro-eye match cut and a 3840×2160 FFV1 master. That was a controlled edit, not a seamless anatomical match: separate lids, catchlights, texture and pupil size still differed.

V2 removed the photographic-eye substitution. Entry and exit used the **same approved portrait**, with the abstract world revealed only inside its projected pupil. This is the selected generated portrait, not a claim that the original photograph was never edited. A moving interior replaced a frozen portal landing. Camera retiming shortened exposure to unsupported macro detail; it could not recover detail absent from the small source.

The stationary source orb was locally suppressed and replaced with a hand-authored travelling leader. The correction was deterministic by original source-frame index. A small soft reconstruction patch and competing pre-existing highlights remained disclosed. The return reused the **actual corrected forward RGB frames in reverse**, instead of independently advancing another signal clock or generating a new return world.

Recorded checks matched 60 reverse/forward processed-frame pairs per aspect. This proves the relevant pre-encode frame reuse, not byte-identical decoded lossy MP4s. Earlier blanket advice against reversing every effect was superseded here by the deliberate v2 choice to retrace the corrected sequence.

**Lesson:** change the architecture when repeated local blends cannot satisfy identity continuity. A version-pinned renderer and source-index mapping matter: an already-running process does not automatically import a newly edited signal module. [V2 renderer][v2-renderer] · [signal freeze and limitations][signal] · [v2 review][v2-review]

## 8. Responsive media is composition work; overlays are a separate layer

**3 October — four reviewed families.** Wide 16:9, tablet 4:3, portrait-tablet 3:4 and phone 9:16 received distinct focal paths and project layouts. Portraits were uniformly projected from the approved source. Hardware scenes used deliberate scene-layer framing and designed dark space; narrow layouts used re-arranged blank planes. This was not stretching a landscape movie or adding rectangular black bars.

A landscape-tablet mode switch exposed a real failure: frame 323→324 jumped vertically when a clamped crop changed to an unclamped field projection. The reverse reproduced it. The correction inherited the preceding camera transform, then eased toward the desired framing. A bounded retest covered the affected forward/reverse window and final showcase; unaffected frame hashes were preserved. Wide/phone and corrected tablet deliveries have separate final review receipts.

Initial plans called for real sanitized screenshots. The shipped website instead uses **labelled project concept/preview covers**, not fabricated application screenshots. Code maps them to recorded per-frame plane quadrilaterals; Attendance ERP occupies the centre. Titles, links and controls stay out of the film. The underlying planes and processor still need geometric review—ordinary HTML cannot automatically track arbitrary generated geometry.

**Lesson:** verify the complete active subject at each aspect and the transform at every mode boundary. Review the overlay in the browser as well as the video without it. [Responsive renderer][responsive] · [tablet repair receipt][tablet] · [project overlays][overlays]

## 9. Public content, résumé and deployment were separate acceptance gates

**3–4 October.** Public copy was derived separately from source material, qualified prototypes/research accurately and anonymized private project names. The one-page, selectable-text résumé and accessible HTML share approved structured content. Historical authoring and repository-access handoffs were not publication evidence; the later résumé-sync status records a normal push and remote verification.

Repository publication also changed course. A parentless local candidate was initially only a proposal. Rewriting history alone was not treated as proof that the existing remote could safely become public. The later migration receipt records explicit approval and a **fresh public repository with a clean root**, while previous history/tooling remained private.

The existing Cloudflare Pages project and domain were preserved and connected to the public GitHub source. The reviewed build allowlists public files and active media into `dist`; it does not upload the project root. Mutable files revalidate; versioned media retain immutable caching. Retired-path/cache failures in older audits were not erased from history or misrepresented as successful purges.

**Lesson:** current-tree hygiene, reachable history, deploy output and cached public URLs are separate surfaces. Approval to build media does not authorize publishing personal records, changing visibility or bypassing repository permissions. ## 10. v5/v6 traded all-I weight for measured adaptive delivery

**3–4 October — historical all-I path, then v5.** Earlier quality-first delivery used large all-I 1080-short-edge files split into transport parts to meet the host's per-asset limit. It preserved that encode's bytes, but did not reduce total transfer cost. Its performance scores, readiness times and chunk architecture do not describe the current release.

V5 selected eight independent MP4 files: balanced and high tiers for four compositions, using bounded 12–24-frame GOPs. The final worker report supersedes the partial pilot handoff. Even the wide balanced file's miss against an initial size aim was explicitly accepted, rather than hidden.

Selection considers useful display raster/DPR, connection and device hints, then a bounded `MediaCapabilities.decodingInfo()` check when available. Reduced motion, data saving and direct content entry avoid automatic movie loading; static content remains usable. Hints and codec support are not guarantees of smooth playback.

V5 also corrected seek-driven guided playback: continuous native video playback with a bounded changing rate replaced seeking on every animation frame during the explicit guided visit. Manual scrolling remained seek-based and interruptible. An offline, smoothed optical-flow proxy informed the source-time curve; it did not measure constant physical camera velocity.

**4 October — v6 desktop/readability refinement.** Only the wide high encode changed: CRF 21 with B2 and GOP 24, from the approved **lossy v2 max source**, not from newly captured detail. The same-resolution SSIM mean improved from approximately 0.98627 to 0.98752 against that reference; this is a spatial comparison, not a perceptual certificate.

The media still lasts 20 seconds. The guided visit uses roughly 25–27 seconds of scroll time, with slightly quicker eye passages and one symmetric project-camera “breath,” not a second reveal or dead hold. The approximately 27-second reference curve allocates 3.4 seconds around projects; two recorded emulated experiences measured about 2.35/2.57 seconds of readable exposure. Do not promise those times for manual scrolling or every device.

**Lesson:** separately version film time, scroll mapping, presentation behaviour and encode settings. GOP duration is not browser seek latency; “below 25 MiB” is a Cloudflare Pages constraint, not a universal video target. [v5 final encode selection][v5-media] · [v5 completion][v5-complete] · [v6 pacing][v6-pacing] · [v6 completion][v6-complete]

## 11. A local sound experiment, then the choice of silence

**4 October — owner chose silence.** Four sparse synthesized accents for iris entry, processor activation, project appearance and return were tried and tested locally. None were published. Hemanth chose to omit sound effects and proceed with the release. The website and final recording are intentionally sound-free; the experimental audio is not a deferred feature awaiting approval.

This records the owner's decision, not a fresh verification of audio removal, the final recording or the next deployment. Those checks need their own release evidence rather than the earlier 48-test baseline. Optional-audio guidance for other portfolio owners remains valid: Hemanth's choice is not a global prohibition.

## Claims to keep qualified

- **Motif origin:** stage 01 records an approved personal concept; no earlier conversation was independently reconstructed here.
- **Model provenance:** the portrait model and early third-party clip routing remain unspecified/user-attributed. Do not infer them from branding.
- **Run scope:** Kaggle completion is established by later receipts; GPU parallelism, exact billing and pilot extrapolated timings are not.
- **Visual scope:** contact sheets, native crops, emulated browsers and processed-frame hashes prove different things. None certifies perfect behaviour on all devices.
- **Silent release:** the owner rejected the locally tested sound experiment on 4 October; no accents were published. Final removal, recording and deployment checks must be dated separately from the pinned baseline.
